# Photo consent form — blank template

**Read the warning in [README.md](README.md) before using this.** Your sport's
national body very likely publishes its own member-protection consent form. If
it does, use theirs instead of this one.

**How to use it:** replace every `[BLANK]`, then either print it for sign-on
day or paste it into an online form. Keep completed forms with your membership
records — not on the website.

**When you need it:** before publishing any photograph of a person under 18 on
the club website, its gallery, or club social media.

---

## Photograph and video consent

We photograph and film club activities — training, games, presentations and
social events — and use them on our website, in our newsletter and on our
social media accounts. They help show what the club is like to families
thinking about joining.

We will not publish images of anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's
permission. This form records that permission, and you can withdraw it at any

### What you're agreeing to

Please tick each one you're happy with. **You can tick some and not others**,
and the player takes part in everything either way.

- [ ] Photos and video of my child may appear on the **club website**
- [ ] Photos and video of my child may appear on the **club's social media**
- [ ] Photos and video of my child may appear in **printed club material**
      (newsletters, programs, signage)
- [ ] My child's **first name** may appear alongside their photo
- [ ] My child's **full name** may appear alongside their photo
- [ ] Photos may be given to **local newspapers or our league** for coverage of
      games and results

### What we promise

Whatever you tick:

- We never publish a child's home address, school, date of birth or contact
  details alongside a photograph.
- We never publish a photograph we think is undignified or embarrassing, even
  if we have permission.
- We only publish images taken at club activities.
- **You can change your mind at any time.** Email `[CLUB EMAIL]` and we will
  remove the images. You do not have to give a reason, and it will never affect
  your child's place in the club.

Because photos may already have been shared or downloaded by others, we cannot
promise to remove every copy that exists elsewhere — only those we control.

## Notes for the committee — delete before printing

**Keeping these forms**

- Store completed forms with your membership records, securely, and **not** on
  the website. This site is not built to hold signed consent forms.
- Keep them for as long as the player is a member.
- Refresh them each season. A form signed for an 8-year-old should not still be
  relied on when they are 15.

**Using them in practice**

- The person who uploads photos to the website needs to know who has consented
  to what. Agree how that gets checked before the first photo goes up — a
  spreadsheet of who has consented is usually enough.
- The safest default, and the one most clubs land on, is **team photos with no
  names, and individual photos only where a form is on file**.
- If a parent has not returned a form, treat that as "no". Silence is not
  permission.

**Practical reality**

At a public ground, spectators and opposition supporters take photographs and
the club cannot control that. This form covers what **the club** publishes.
Say so plainly if a parent asks, rather than promising more than you can
deliver.
