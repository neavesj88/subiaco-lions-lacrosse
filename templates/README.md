# Blank forms and documents

Fill-in-the-blanks starting points for a club setting this website up. They are
**not** part of the website and nothing here is published automatically —
these are documents your committee fills in, keeps, or copies into the site.

Everything is blank and generic. No club's real details appear in any of them.

| File | What it's for | When you need it |
|---|---|---|
| [club-details-worksheet.md](club-details-worksheet.md) | Every detail the site will ask you for, in one list | Before you start setup |
| [privacy-policy.md](privacy-policy.md) | A privacy policy to adapt and publish | Before you take any sign-ups |
| [photo-consent-form.md](photo-consent-form.md) | Permission to publish photos of a child | Before any junior photo goes on the site |
| [committee-handover-checklist.md](committee-handover-checklist.md) | Passing website access to next year's committee | At each AGM |

## How to use these

Anything written like `[CLUB NAME]` is a blank for you to fill in. Work through
a file top to bottom and replace every one — searching for `[` will find any
you missed.

Copy a file first and edit the copy, so you always have the blank original if
you need to start again:

```bash
cp templates/privacy-policy.md our-privacy-policy.md
```

## An honest warning about two of these

`privacy-policy.md` and `photo-consent-form.md` deal with the law and with
children. They are a **starting point written in plain English, not legal
advice**, and no website template can know your club's circumstances,
insurer's requirements, or your state or national association's rules.

Before you publish either one:

- Read it against your sport's own member-protection and child-safe policies —
  most national bodies publish templates that override anything here.
- If your club is incorporated, or handles significant money, have someone on
  the committee with the relevant background read it. Many associations offer
  this free to affiliated clubs.
- The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) publishes
  free plain-English guidance on what a privacy policy must contain.

Using these unchanged is better than having nothing. Using them unchecked is
not the same as having taken advice.
