# Privacy policy — blank template

**Read the warning in [README.md](README.md) before publishing this.** It is a
plain-English starting point, not legal advice, and your sport's national body
almost certainly publishes one that takes precedence.

**How to use it:** replace every `[BLANK]`, delete any section that doesn't
apply to your club, then paste the result into the website under
**Admin → Pages → Add page**, titled "Privacy". Link it from your footer.

---

## Privacy Policy

**[CLUB NAME]**
Last updated: `[DATE]`

### Who we are

[CLUB NAME] is a `[incorporated association / club]` based at `[GROUND, SUBURB,
STATE]`. You can contact us about anything in this policy at `[CLUB EMAIL]`.

### What we collect

When you use this website we may collect:

- **When you enquire about joining:** your name, email address, phone number,
  date of birth, playing experience, and which team you're interested in.
- **For players under 18:** the same, plus a parent or guardian's name, email
  and phone number.
- **When you contact us:** your name, email address and your message.
- **When you subscribe to club news:** your name and email address.
- **When you buy something from our shop:** your name, email, phone and, if you
  ask us to post it, your postal address.

We do **not** collect anything about you simply for visiting the website. This
site uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

### Why we collect it

Only to run the club:

- to contact you about joining, training times, fixtures and club events
- to register players with `[LEAGUE OR ASSOCIATION NAME]` as our competition
  requires
- to fulfil and deliver anything you order from the club shop
- to send club news, if you asked us to

We do not use your details for anything else, and we never sell them.

### Who else sees it

- **`[LEAGUE OR ASSOCIATION NAME]`**, for player registration and eligibility.
- **Our email provider**, which delivers our messages to you.
- **Stripe**, if you buy something from our shop. Stripe handles the payment on
  their own systems — the club never sees or stores your card details.

That is the complete list. We do not share your details with anyone else unless
you ask us to, or unless we are required to by law.

### Children's information

We take particular care with information about people under 18:

- We ask for a parent or guardian's contact details for every junior player, so
  there is always a responsible adult we can reach.
- A parent or guardian must be the one who provides a child's details to us.
- Junior members' details are visible only to the committee members who need
  them, and are never published on this website, in search results, or anywhere
  public.
- **We do not publish a child's full name alongside their photograph** without
  the written permission of their parent or guardian. See our photo consent
  process at `[WHERE — e.g. "on request from the club secretary"]`.

### Photographs

We photograph club activities for the website and social media. If you or your
child appear in a photo and you would rather you didn't, tell us at
`[CLUB EMAIL]` and we will remove it. We do not need a reason.

### How long we keep it

- Enquiries that don't become memberships: `[e.g. deleted at the end of the
  season]`
- Member details: for as long as you are a member, and `[e.g. two years]`
  afterwards in case you return
- Shop orders: `[e.g. seven years]`, because we are required to keep financial
  records
- Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe

### How we look after it

The website runs on `[e.g. a computer at our clubhouse / a server we rent]`.
Access needs a password, and only committee members we have given accounts to
can see any of the details above. We back the information up regularly.

We are a volunteer-run club, not a large organisation, and we are honest about
that: we take reasonable care, but we cannot promise perfect security. Please
do not send us anything highly sensitive through this website.

### Seeing or deleting your information

Email `[CLUB EMAIL]` and ask. You can:

- ask what we hold about you or your child
- ask us to correct anything wrong
- ask us to delete it

We will action it within `[e.g. 30 days]`. There is no charge, and asking will
never affect your membership.

### Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your information, tell us first at
`[CLUB EMAIL]` — we would rather fix it. If you are not satisfied with our
response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information
Commissioner at **oaic.gov.au**.

### Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change
the date at the top.

## Notes for whoever fills this in — delete this section before publishing

- **Retention periods are the section people invent numbers for.** Ask your
  association what they require before writing anything in. Financial records
  in Australia generally need keeping for several years; check rather than
  guess.
- **The "who else sees it" list must be true and complete.** If you use a
  registration platform, a team messaging app, or a coaching tool that holds
  member details, it belongs on that list.
- If your club is **not** incorporated, or turns over less than $3 million, the
  Privacy Act may not strictly apply to you. Publishing a policy anyway is
  still the right thing to do, and parents increasingly expect it.
- The website's sign-up form shows a short privacy notice and requires people to
  tick that they've read it. Edit that wording under **Admin → Settings** so it
  matches this policy — the two disagreeing is worse than either alone.
